Who We Are

Two Companies,
Shared Operations

Nebraska-based refrigerated freight specialists. We haul fresh & frozen goods across 15+ states — temp-controlled, on-time, every load.

Freight Brokerage

Friend Logistics LLC

Friend Logistics is a federally authorized freight broker specializing in refrigerated freight. We coordinate temp-controlled loads for fresh and frozen commodities across our Midwest, Southern, and Western lanes — matching reliable carriers with shippers who need it done right.

Transportation Operations

JBJ Transportation LLC

JBJ Transportation provides the on-the-ground reefer operations that make our logistics work. Working alongside Friend Logistics, JBJ keeps drivers supported with direct communication, dispatch, and the resources needed to deliver temp-controlled freight safely — every single load.
